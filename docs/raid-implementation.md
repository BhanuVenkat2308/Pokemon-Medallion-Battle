# Solo raid expeditions — implemented 2026-11-03

Source: [Bulbapedia Pokémon Medallion Battle](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_Medallion_Battle#Raids). The species table is source-based; the solo economy, weekly schedule and expanded expeditions are approved recreation rules.

## Rotation and access

- Permanent unlock at all 11 badges OR best 6,000 RP.
- Personal 168-hour weeks from unlock, rotating all 25 expeditions then looping. Offline weeks advance without free rewards.
- Each week starts stage 1 with zero damage. An existing attempt may finish its old week and earn that week’s reward before rotation.
- Six passes maximum, one regenerated per 30 minutes. Starting costs one; reloading/resuming does not.

## Combat and rewards

- Fixed featured boss plus five distinct families from a ten-family pool. At least two red, at most two Ultra; existing gym quotas apply.
- Stage bands 1–4 / 5–7 / 8–12 / 13+ use support IV 0–10 / 8–18 / 16–25 / 26–30 and boss IV 10 / 18 / 25 / 30. Actual CP, powers and legality guide form selection.
- Raid enemy leads, with existing Protect exception. First loss ends the attempt; one 40-Candy revive when both benches still have available Pokémon.
- Win damage = max(100, final player CP minus final opponent CP); losses and Protect deal zero. Damage saves immediately.
- Boss HP stages 1–12: 1,000, 1,500, 2,500, 4,000, 5,500, 7,000, 9,000, 11,000, 13,000, 15,000, 17,000, 19,000. Stage 13+: 20,000.
- Clearing immediately ends the attempt, adds one fixed-content pack plus 300 Coins and 100 Candy, and starts the next stage with no overflow damage.
- Existing attempt rewards: 30 + 20 per won confrontation + 60 for an attempt win; 20 Candy for a win or 10 otherwise. No RP changes.

## Raid Packs

| Stages | Raid slots | Gym slots | Featured legendary per pack | Shiny per card | Other IVs |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| 1–4 | 2 | 3 | 0% | 2% | 0–30 |
| 5–7 | 2 | 3 | 20% | 2% | 0–30 |
| 8–12 | 3 | 2 | 25% | 2% | 0–30 |
| 13+ | 3 | 2 | 30% | 15% | 26–30 |

One legendary roll, at most one featured boss, consuming a raid slot. Remaining raid slots draw uniformly with replacement from the listed non-legends, including the starter. Gym slots draw uniformly from unlocked regular initial forms excluding all starters and legendaries. All five receive the raid IV rules. Shiny means IV31; species base CP never changes.

Packs are rolled at earning time and retained in an inbox across weeks. Claiming needs five free bag spaces. No standard-pack rarity guarantees, starter cap, latest-gym guarantee or standard Shiny odds apply to Raid Packs. Standard, Silver, daily and welcome pack rules and pools are unchanged.

## Persistence and rollback

Version-2 raid state migrates old test raids to stage 1 without changing the collection, balances, RP or badges. It saves weekly timestamps, damage, passes, opponent forms/powers/IVs, selections, round results, effects, revive state and reward ledgers. The result is committed before animation.

Source snapshot: `backups/before-raids-2026-11-03/`. Reverting code after collecting newly added species also requires a compatible pre-update save export; do not load an expanded roster save into the old build. Export your save before reverting.
