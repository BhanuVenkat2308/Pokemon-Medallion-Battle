# Current roster audit

Verified 2026-10-30 against [Bulbapedia](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_Medallion_Battle#Special_Pok%C3%A9mon). All Special Pokémon, Gym, and Raid species tables were checked. Tier values were read from the Tier-column image filenames, not inferred from CP or evolution.

62 of the 71 current species have matching entries. The other nine retain their immediately preceding values and are explicitly unverified. No species were removed or newly added.

These source values supersede the custom rarity CP formula and CP-based ball classification. Eevee and its branches return to rarity 3. IV, saved powers, ownership, discovery, RP gates, pack Shiny odds, and team restrictions are preserved. Evolution Candy costs use the subsequently approved custom schedule in README.md; source CP, tier, and rarity remain as listed. Existing teams that exceed tier limits load intact but must be adjusted before play.
